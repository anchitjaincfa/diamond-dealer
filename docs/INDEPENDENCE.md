# Independence policy

The customer pays for a structured review, not for a sale.

The Diamond Dealer demo:

- takes no referral fee, sales commission, placement payment, or lead fee from sellers;
- does not sell preferred outcomes or seller suppression rights;
- does not rank sellers for compensation;
- accepts no gifts, inventory, travel, or review incentives from interested parties;
- separates price context from seller advertising;
- requires disclosure of relevant financial, family, and employment relationships.

A reviewer with a conflict must recuse. A sponsor may support the software only if it cannot access customer reports, reviewer assignments, methods, or outcomes. Future commercial relationships that change these statements should be disclosed before taking effect.

Corrections, revocations, and appeal results remain visible because independence also requires a record that can withstand inconvenient updates.
