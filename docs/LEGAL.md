# Demo and legal notice

This repository and deployed site are a product simulation built with fictional people, sellers, listings, documents, events, and prices. It does not accept real orders, inspect physical items, or process payments.

The output is **not verified**, **not certified**, **not guaranteed**, **not appraised**, and does not establish that an item is **authentic** or **worth** any amount. No report says an item is **safe to buy**. It is not legal, tax, investment, insurance, gemological, or purchasing advice.

A favorable demo outcome means only that supplied synthetic material was internally consistent within a stated scope and date. Images and documents can be incomplete, altered, stale, or unrelated to a physical item. Seller signals are indicators of uncertainty, not findings of intent, identity, or wrongdoing.

Real decisions should account for payment protections, written return terms, insured delivery, jurisdiction, current independent physical inspection, and qualified professional advice appropriate to the transaction.

All names and scenarios are invented. Any resemblance to a real person, business, listing, report, or event is coincidental.
