# Customer and report policies

These policies describe proposed behavior shown by the demo. No real orders or payments are processed.

## 24-hour target

The simulated service targets an initial outcome within 24 hours after a complete submission. The clock starts when the required listing capture and readable supporting material are present. This is a target, not a promise.

If a short delay may resolve a material question, the customer receives a specific extension, new target, reason, and choice to wait or close with a refund.

## Refunds

The proposed $49 fee is refunded when core questions are not assessable, the listing is already unavailable, an accepted target is missed before issue, or the customer declines an offered extension. Closure remains visible. Disagreement with a properly scoped outcome uses the appeal process instead.

## One appeal

One appeal may be requested within seven calendar days. It must identify the disputed conclusion and relevant old or new evidence. A different reviewer considers that scoped question. While pending, the report remains visible with a notice. The result may uphold, amend, supersede, void, or revoke it.

## Extensions

An extension states why more time could help, the new target, and customer options. Silence is not acceptance. The proposed standard workflow offers no more than one extension.

## Voiding and supersession

A report maps to a captured listing version. Material changes to the item, price structure, seller identity, condition, documents, or terms may void it. Material new evidence may instead produce a versioned replacement. Earlier versions remain readable and point forward.

## Revocation

A material fraud signal, source failure, data mix-up, or reviewer error may trigger revocation. The notice withdraws the earlier outcome without unsupported accusations, gives the evidence category and time, and explains any replacement or refund path.

## Privacy concept

A production service should collect only what a review needs, set deletion periods, encrypt uploads and logs, and provide export and deletion paths. This demo uses synthetic data and must not request payment details, government identifiers, home addresses, or confidential correspondence.
