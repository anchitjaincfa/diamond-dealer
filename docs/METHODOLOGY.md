# Review methodology

The Diamond Dealer is a public product demo for an independent, evidence-led second look at engagement-ring and diamond listings. Every example is synthetic.

## Review question

What do the supplied materials support, contradict, or leave unknown as of the review date?

The review does not inspect a physical item, identify unknown material from an image, or predict future prices or resale outcomes.

## Evidence order

1. Complete, legible primary documents tied to the item by matching references.
2. Dated images or video showing relevant details and provenance.
3. The captured listing and written seller disclosures.
4. Customer-provided messages and transaction terms.
5. Broad comparison data, used only for descriptive price context.

A formal-looking source is not automatically stronger. Scope, date, legibility, internal consistency, and connection to the item all matter.

## Review dimensions

- **Specification consistency:** Compare stated shape, weight, measurements, color and clarity descriptions, treatments, setting metal, and references. Name differences instead of silently reconciling them.
- **Price context:** Use a dated comparison set with similar stated attributes. Present ranges and major differences, not false precision. Separate stone, setting, services, taxes, and shipping where possible.
- **Seller signals:** Review visible identity, contact consistency, return and shipping terms, payment pressure, image reuse, edits, and contradictions. Signals affect uncertainty; they do not prove intent.
- **Evidence gaps:** Record missing, cropped, stale, illegible, or untethered material. Unknowns remain unknown.

## Outcomes

- **Issued - consistent:** Compared material agrees on in-scope questions, subject to stated limits.
- **Issued - caveats:** A useful report is possible, but conflicts or gaps remain.
- **Not assessable - refunded:** Evidence is too limited to answer core questions.
- **Superseded by upgrade:** Material new evidence produced a replacement.
- **Voided - listing changed:** The captured review no longer maps to the live offer.
- **Revoked - fraud signal:** A later signal makes the earlier outcome unsuitable for reliance.
- **Revoked - reviewer error:** A material review mistake was found and disclosed.
- **Extension available:** A short delay may resolve a material question; the customer chooses.
- **Appeal pending:** A scoped challenge is being considered by another reviewer.

## Reviewer discipline

Every material conclusion identifies its source or is labeled as an inference. Reviewers disclose uncertainty, preserve a dated listing snapshot, and keep lifecycle events append-only. Corrections are not hidden by overwriting history.

Before issue, check identifiers, dates, item-to-source matching, prominent conflicts, dated price context, working links, policy events, and whether the summary stays within the evidence.
